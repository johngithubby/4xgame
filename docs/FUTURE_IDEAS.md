# Future Game Ideas

This document captures uncommitted future feature ideas for later design passes. These are brainstorm notes, not promises for the current prototype scope.

## Base Building & HQ Progression

- The HQ should increase in size as it upgrades.
- The HQ should change color as it upgrades.
- The player should be able to zoom in and out of the base.
- The base should need regular defense from hordes of enemies.
- Base defense could include wall construction, moats, and other physical barriers.

## Human Rescue & Chemical Warfare

- A base can build chemical warfare research labs.
- Research labs let the player design chemical bombs or chemical drones.
- Chemical weapons can slowly dezombify zombies.
- Chemical potency should be directly proportional to the time required for a zombie to become human again.
- Once a zombie becomes human again, they should stop in shock and look at their hands and feet.
- Rescued humans should panic when they see nearby zombies.
- Zombies should attack rescued humans.
- If the squad kills these restored humans, the player loses coins.
- At the end of the round, saved humans should grant more credits.
- Rescued humans should be the main way the number of humans in the base increases.
- Rescued humans can arrive at the base gates after a mission.
- The player can let rescued humans into the base so they become members.
- New base members can be trained into roles such as soldiers, engineers, workers, and other specialists.

## Drones, Aviation & Recon

- Bases can train drone operators.
- Drones should require metals, engineers, workers, and fuel.
- Aviation can include fighters, bombers, recon aircraft, and helicopters.
- Helicopters can support attack and recon roles.
- Recon aviation can scout enemy herds before they reach the base.
- Before a battle, the player can send drones, missiles, or bombs to soften up a herd.

## Satellites & Strategic Intelligence

- Satellites can discover world resources.
- Satellites can discover enemy facilities.
- Satellites can discover enemy herds.

## Resource Harvesting & Expeditions

- Resource harvesting is done with trucks.
- The player discovers resources outside the base by sending trucks on expeditions.
- Trucks can be specialized for one resource type, such as metal ores or uranium.
- A specialized truck should only search for the resource type chosen for that truck.
- As a truck progresses through the world, it discovers terrain along its path.
- When a truck discovers a resource, extraction work starts automatically.
- Resource extraction should take a short while before the truck begins its return trip.
- The truck returns to the base and dumps its load through a special base opening.
- After unloading, the truck automatically returns to the same resource site.
- The harvest, return, unload, and repeat loop continues until the resource is exhausted.
- The player should only need to choose and send a truck type; the rest of the route and harvesting loop runs automatically.

## Weapons & Escalation

- Weapons can progress from small arms to heavier systems.
- Heavy weapons can include howitzers.
- Heavy weapons can include HIMARS-style rocket artillery.
- Heavy weapons can include tanks.
- Late-game superweapons can include earthquake triggers.
- Late-game superweapons can include weather weaponization.
- Atomic bombs can exist as a major escalation path.
- Atomic bombs require harvesting uranium.
- Atomic bombs require engineers.
- Atomic bombs require launch vehicles, such as submarines, airplane bombers, or ground-launched ballistic missiles.

## Zombie Evolution & Counterplay

- Zombies can pile on top of each other to become tall enough to overcome physical obstructions like walls.
- Tall zombie piles can attack helicopters or drones.
- Zombies can defend against drones by holding or wearing large boulders overhead.
- Hero zombies are bigger, smarter, and harder to kill.
- Hero zombies can assemble rudimentary armor.
- Zombie categories can include irrational foot soldiers that are easy to kill.
- Zombie categories can include armored zombies.
- Zombie categories can include rock-throwing zombies.

## Squad Command & Tactics

- A squad can be divided into groups.
- Tapping members of a group selects that group.
- Selected groups can have their fire directed by tapping enemies.
- The player can designate group positions and roles, such as rear guard or attack.

## Herd Management & Base Threats

- The player can cull herds before they reach the base.
- Culling herds can reduce the risk of the base being overrun.
- Recon aviation can scout incoming enemy herds before base attacks.
